Terrain Staves GEMCRAFT

18 elemental staves × 6 tiers — reshape terrain, buff yourself, and bend the world.

Terrain Staves are elemental wands — one per type, each in six material tiers. Every staff has a passive effect while held, a right-click action, and a shift + right-click action. Higher tiers scale range, area, power and duration.

Tiers craft from the matching material (stick → cobblestone → iron → gold → diamond); the Netherite staff is a smithing upgrade.

“+1 potion level” means the Gold and Netherite staves grant their potion-style buffs one level stronger — e.g. Speed II instead of Speed I, Resistance II instead of Resistance I.
