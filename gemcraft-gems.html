Type Gems are the core reagent of GemCraft. There is one gem for each of the 18 elemental types, and they feed almost every system in the mod.

What gems are used for

  • Terrain Staves — every staff is crafted around the gem of its type.
  • Aura Linkers — a gem attunes a linker (and its portal) to a type’s colour.
  • Serum infusion — dropping a gem into a Chimera Serum infuses that type’s effect.
  • Ground Staff transform — hold a gem while using a Ground staff to reshape terrain into that type’s theme.
  • Psychic Staff biome-seeking — the gem sets which biome the seeker orb hunts for.
