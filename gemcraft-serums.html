Serums & Potions GEMCRAFT

Brew type serums for lasting buffs — balance the stability meter or the stand blows up.

Serums are brewed, drinkable buffs. You start from a base Chimera Serum, infuse it with Type Gems to add effects, and manage a stability meter as you go. Push it far enough and you can brew the all-powerful Omni Serum.

The brewing chain

  • Chimera Serum — brew a Potion of Regeneration with Blaze Powder. The raw base (also gives Nausea).
  • Infused Serum — brew a Type Gem into a serum to add that type’s effect. One recipe handles all 18 types.
  • Omni Serum — the fully-infused serum: every effect at once for 30 minutes.
The stability puzzle. A serum starts at 50 stability (max 100). Infusing a type your serum is weak to costs stability; a super-effective type adds a little back. Drop below zero and the brew fails — and, if enabled, the brewing stand explodes. Infusing a Normal gem wipes the serum clean and resets it, letting you start the sequence over.
Omni Serum — the catch. It grants all 15 effects for a full 30 minutes, but when it finally wears off it kills you. Powerful, but plan your exit.

Grimer potions

Separate from serums, GemCraft adds Grimer / Alolan Grimer / Sludge / Toxic Sludge potions. The sludge potions are drinkable (harmful effects); the Grimer potions are throwable splash projectiles.

Type: Null serum

A special serum tied to the Type: Null system — a burst of Absorption and Resistance at the cost of heavy Slowness and Mining Fatigue while it lasts.
