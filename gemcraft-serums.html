Serums are brewed, drinkable buffs: a base Chimera Serum infused with Type Gems for elemental effects, up to the fully-infused Omni Serum.

The brewing chain

  • Chimera Serum — brew a Potion of Regeneration with Blaze Powder. The raw base (also gives Nausea).
  • Infused Serum — brew a Type Gem into a serum to add that type’s effect. One recipe handles all 18 types.
  • Omni Serum — the fully-infused serum.

Grimer potions

Separate from serums, GemCraft adds Grimer / Alolan Grimer / Sludge / Toxic Sludge potions. The sludge potions are drinkable (harmful effects); the Grimer potions are throwable splash projectiles.

Type: Null serum

A special serum tied to the Type: Null system — a burst of Absorption and Resistance at the cost of heavy Slowness and Mining Fatigue while it lasts.
