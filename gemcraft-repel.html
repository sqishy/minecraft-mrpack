Repel blocks are placeable beacons that keep wild Pokémon from spawning nearby. Set one down at your base, farm, or arena and the surrounding area stays quiet — no more spawns crowding the spot while it’s placed.

How it works

  • Cubic radius. Each tier suppresses spawns within a fixed block radius of the block — Repel 48, Super Repel 96, Max Repel 144. (Ranges are server-configurable.)
  • Stops hostile mobs too. By default a Repel also blocks vanilla hostile-monster spawns in its radius, the way a lit area does. Passive animals are unaffected.
  • Fishing still works. Spawns you deliberately reel in while fishing are never blocked, even standing right on top of a Repel.
  • Right-click to check. Right-click in the air with a Repel in hand to find out whether another Repel is already nearby.
  • Only while placed. Break the block and its zone disappears immediately.

Crafting

Three tiers, each a wider zone than the last. Higher tiers are crafted by upgrading the previous tier — you never lose the block you put in.
