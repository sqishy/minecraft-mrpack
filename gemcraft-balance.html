Balance Changes GEMCRAFT

A dex-wide movepool & ability overhaul — mostly from Pokémon Unbound, plus our custom legendary kit.

OverviewType GemsTerrain StavesAura PortalsAssembly BlocksSerums & PotionsLiving EnchantmentFossil RevivalDungeonsBalance ChangesBattle RulesRepel Block

GemCraft ships a sweeping competitive rebalance — expanded movepools and reworked abilities across almost the entire Pokédex. The bulk of these buffs are ported from the Pokémon Unbound rom hack; the custom signature moves and abilities we wrote ourselves (mostly for legendaries) are tagged GemCraft. Tap an Unbound card to see its exact changes.
