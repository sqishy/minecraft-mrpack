Balance Changes GEMCRAFT

A dex-wide movepool & ability overhaul — Unbound for regular Pokémon, custom kits for legendaries.

OverviewTerrain StavesAura PortalsAssembly BlocksSerums & PotionsFossil RevivalDungeonsBalance ChangesBattle RulesRepel Block

GemCraft ships a sweeping competitive rebalance — expanded movepools and reworked abilities across almost the entire Pokédex. The buffs for regular Pokémon are ported from the Pokémon Unbound rom hack; the legendaries we tuned ourselves (with custom signature moves and abilities) are tagged GemCraft.

DarkraiGemCraft

GiratinaGemCraft
Signature move Shadow Force (Ghost · Physical · 100 BP) — A same-turn attack that hits both foes and breaks through Protect. Giratina's signature.
Ho-OhGemCraft
Signature move Sacred Fire (Fire · Physical · 100 BP) — A physical Fire move that strikes the target's Special Defense, with a 50% chance to burn. Ho-Oh's signature.

Signature ability Portal Power — Non-contact moves used against the holder deal 25% less damage. Hoopa Unbound's ability.

Signature ability Turboblaze — The holder's moves ignore the target's ability, and its Fire moves get +30% damage. Reshiram's ability.
LugiaGemCraft
Signature move Aeroblast (Flying · Special · 100 BP) — Always lands a guaranteed critical hit, bypassing the target's defense boosts and screens. Lugia's signature.
Signature move Silver Gale (Flying · Special · 80 BP) — A special Flying attack whose damage scales off the user's Special Defense instead of Special Attack.
MewtwoGemCraft
Signature move Psystrike (Psychic · Special · 100 BP) — Uses the user's higher attacking stat but always deals damage against the target's Defense. Mewtwo's signature.
Signature ability Neural Surge — Sets Psychic Terrain on switch-in and boosts the holder's super-effective hits by 25% while it's up. Mewtwo's ability.
PalkiaGemCraft
Signature ability Spatial Collapse — Keeps Gravity active the whole time the holder is on the field. Palkia's ability.
ReshiramGemCraft

Signature ability Turboblaze — The holder's moves ignore the target's ability, and its Fire moves get +30% damage. Reshiram's ability.
SolgaleoGemCraft
Signature move Sunsteel Strike (Steel · Physical · 100 BP) — Hits both foes and ignores their abilities, with a 20% chance to lower Defense. Solgaleo's signature.
