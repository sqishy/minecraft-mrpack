GemCraft adds themed dungeons scattered through the world. Each one is a self-contained challenge packed with trainers to battle and valuable rewards to claim if you make it through.

Getting in

Base dungeons are locked, and you open them with a Dungeon Key. Keys are crafted (starting from a Tera Shard and a metal) and come in tiers — higher-tier keys open tougher dungeons with better rewards.

Tier 1 Key

Crafted from a Tera Shard and copper — opens the first-tier dungeons.

Tier 2 Key

A sturdier key for the tier-2 dungeons.

Tier 3 Key

Opens the tier-3 dungeons.

What’s inside

  • Trainers — skilled opponents to battle your way past.
  • Rewards — valuable loot for clearing the challenge.
Bring a healthy, well-prepared team — dungeons are meant to test it.
