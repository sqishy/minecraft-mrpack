Aura Portals GEMCRAFT

Obsidian frames + single-use Aura Linkers = colour-coded, linked teleport portals.

Aura Portals are player-built teleporters: an obsidian frame ignited with an Aura Linker opens a colour-coded gateway.

Mechanics

  • Frame: obsidian, same shape rules as a Nether portal.
  • The Aura Linker is single-use and attuned to one type (from the Type Gem used to craft it), which sets the portal’s colour.
  • Igniting a second frame with a matching linker links the pair.
  • Entering either portal teleports to its partner (short cooldown between trips).
Portals come in all 18 type colours; attunement is cosmetic only:

normalfirewaterelectricgrassicefightingpoisongroundflyingpsychicbugrockghostdragondarksteelfairy

Crafting the Aura Linker

Ender pearls + a Type Gem + an Omni Serum + an echo shard.
