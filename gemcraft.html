GemCraft is a content addon layered on top of Cobblemon: craftable and "assembled" Pokémon, an elemental gem economy, terrain staves, aura portals, brewable serums, themed dungeons, and a dex-wide battle rebalance.

Terrain Staves

108 Terrain Staves — 18 types × 6 tiers — that reshape the world.

Aura Portals

Link obsidian frames into colour-coded teleport portals.

Assembly Blocks

Craft-and-place blocks that assemble artificial Pokémon.

Serums & Potions

Brewable type serums for timed elemental buffs.

Fossil Revival

Revive Mew, Mewtwo and Type: Null from fossils & DNA.

Pokémon Drops

Dungeons

Themed dungeons with trainer battles and rewards, opened by tiered keys.

Dungeon Eggs

Balance Changes

A dex-wide movepool & ability overhaul (from Pokémon Unbound).

Battle Rules

Global battle rules: perfect IVs, level-50 PvP, legendary limits.

A block that keeps wild spawns away (currently disabled).
