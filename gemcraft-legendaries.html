Mega Showdown’s legendary transformation items — primal orbs, fusion devices and origin keys — are now craftable from Type Gems, Tera Shards, Z-Crystals and a few themed odds and ends. 17 recipes. Hover any slot to see the ingredient.

Primal Reversion

Ancient orbs that snap the weather trio back to their Primal forms. Built around a blank Mega Stone ringed in Type Gems and Tera Shards, with a bucket of the Pokémon’s element for flavour. Give the orb to the Pokémon as its held item.

Necrozma — Fusion & Ultra Burst

The prism Pokémon’s three keys. The fusion devices are built on the plain elemental Z-Crystals surrounded in obsidian; the Ultranecrozium Z fuses both together.

N Solarizer
→

Fuses Necrozma with Solgaleo → Dusk Mane Necrozma.

  • 4× Obsidian
  • 4× Psychic Tera Shard
  • 1× Steelium-Z
N Lunarizer
→

Fuses Necrozma with Lunala → Dawn Wings Necrozma.

  • 4× Obsidian
  • 4× Psychic Tera Shard
  • 1× Ghostium-Z
Ultranecrozium-Z
→

Lets a fused Necrozma Ultra Burst into Ultra Necrozma (also powers up Photon Geyser).

  • 4× Psychic Tera Shard
  • 1× Steelium-Z
  • 2× Psychic Gem
  • 1× Nether Star
  • 1× Ghostium-Z
